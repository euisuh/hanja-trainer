# Unit 12 gaps (전화·LINE)

Wanted by the curriculum, no suitable Tatoeba sentence (CC BY 2.0 FR / CC0). Not invented. Searched 2026-10-06.

- 折り返しお願いします (learner asks for a call back): no hit. Closest is 折り返し電話をくれ。 (#11455301), a blunt imperative, rejected. Substituted 後ほど私に電話をするよう彼女に伝えていただけますか？ (#774500) for leaving a message, and 折り返し電話します。 (#2063335) / 折り返しご連絡いたします。 (#8424881) for the callback itself.
- 〜名で (reservation under a name, 〜名義で): no hit. Substituted ナムと申します。 (#14048807, same key as unit 7) answering お名前をお願いします。 (#226565).
- 〜時に (as a stand-alone time phrase): only inside full request sentences. Shipped ６時に４人予約したいのですが。 (#235060) and 6時半、4人席を予約したいんですが。 (#11906621).
- 今着いた (exact): no hit. Only 今着いたの？ (#9732827), 今着いたところよ。 (#11545589, feminine ending) and 今駅に着いたわ。 (#172532, feminine ending). Shipped 駅に着いたよ。 (#158266) as the neutral casual substitute.
- ちょっと遅れる (exact): shipped as part of ごめんちょっと遅れる。3時10分には着くと思う。 (#2904021), a two-sentence text with a time; cannot be split.
- 電話番号 (learner gives a number): 電話番号は9876-5432です。 (#4519412) exists, but the digit reading is not unambiguous and kana must be digit-free; not shipped. The hear side 電話番号を伺えますか？ (#774502) is shipped as a chunk but is not used in a scene because the learner has no number sentence to answer with.
- お名前頂戴できますか (exact): no hit. Substituted お名前をお願いします。 (#226565).
- 〜はただいま席を外しております: only 〜は今、席を外しております。 (ただいま absent). Shipped 田中は今、席を外しております。 (#124875) and 申し訳ありません。田中は今、席を外しております。 (#889706). Native-speaker ownership of these two was not checked beyond the CC BY 2.0 FR licence; owner to confirm.
- 少々お待ちください: reused unit 5 chunk (#8886173, key tatoeba-8886173-hear), not duplicated. Not used in a scene: after a reservation request the learner has nothing to say, so the scenes would not end on a learner turn.
- もしもし (answering a call as the learner): shipped only the bare もしもし。 (#385517). 〜と申します exists only as the name-specific ナムと申します。; no generic form.
- LINE-specific text (スタンプ, 既読, 〜ね！ style short messages): out of scope for Tatoeba; the casual lines are spoken-register sentences used as message text.
- The unit has 20 say and 14 hear chunks; the curriculum asked for 20-30 say and 10-20 hear. Say side is at the floor because phone-specific learner lines are scarce.
- The reservation scene has no date. Date sentences (日にち, 〜日の〜時) exist only inside long dialogue texts (#2863731) and cannot be split under the no-excerpt rule.
