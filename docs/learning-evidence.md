# Learning method: evidence and product choices

This app uses research-supported learning principles without claiming that its
exact interface or schedule has been clinically or educationally validated.

## From evidence to UI

| Observed gap | Product choice | Evidence | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home promoted a new lesson while reviews were due. | Make due review the primary action; each session requests at most 20 scheduled character questions. The displayed total remains the whole backlog, and contextual compound extras may appear. | [Kim & Webb (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12479) synthesized 48 L2 spacing experiments (3,411 participants); [Cepeda et al. (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354) synthesized distributed-practice experiments. | Neither review establishes 20 as ideal. Gap effects depend on the later test, so FSRS is retained rather than replaced with a universal interval. |
| Multiple-choice recognition can succeed without producing the answer. | Scheduled review and previous-level review ask learners to attempt recall, reveal the answer, then self-check as **기억하지 못함** or **정확히 기억함**; multiple choice remains available before reveal. Lessons and assessments retain their existing format. | [Karpicke & Roediger (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1152408) found repeated retrieval improved delayed recall of foreign-language word pairs after initial learning. [Kang et al. (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1080/09541440601056620) compared short-answer and multiple-choice retrieval of prose facts and found corrective feedback moderated their later effects. | A self-check is not verified pronunciation or handwriting. The studies did not test this interface or Hanja/Kanji/Hanzi learning, and they do not show that multiple choice is useless. |
| A miss disclosed the answer but ordinary review did not require another attempt in the same session. | Show the answer, then add one ungraded corrective attempt after about three other original questions, or at the remaining tail. It creates no XP, review log, or FSRS update. | [Butler & Roediger (2008)](https://doi.org/10.3758/MC.36.3.604) found feedback enhanced benefits and reduced negative effects of multiple-choice testing. [Rawson & Dunlosky (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023956) found substantial durable-learning gains from spaced successive relearning of conceptual material. | “Three questions” is a local pacing heuristic, not an experimentally optimal interval. One corrective success is practice, not proof of mastery. |
| Isolated character choices offer limited application practice. | Keep existing authentic example sentences, compound cards, mixed reading/meaning prompts, and optional speaking practice. | Retrieval can transfer beyond repeated questions in prose learning ([Butler, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0019902)). | Conceptual inference transfer is not direct evidence of better reading, writing, exams, or speech in this app. Speaking practice is not automatically scored. |

## A simple personal routine

1. Do due reviews first. A backlog may take several sessions; a new lesson is
   still available when desired.
2. Before revealing the answer, say it aloud or write it. If it did not come to
   mind, reveal the answer and choose **기억하지 못함** rather than guessing;
   choose **정확히 기억함** only after comparing your attempt with the answer.
3. Inspect the correction and make the later corrective attempt, then let FSRS
   schedule another-day retrieval instead of repeating until it merely feels easy.
4. Read the authentic example and compound; optionally use speaking practice to
   apply Japanese or Chinese aloud.

Judge improvement by honest, same-mode first-attempt recall on later days—not
by XP, streak, or response speed. Current aggregate accuracy mixes formats and
self-reports, so it is not an objective measure of unaided production.

## What is not established

The cited work mostly studied university learners, word pairs, L2 vocabulary,
or prose. It supports retrieval, corrective feedback, and spaced relearning as
general principles. It does not validate this app's FSRS parameters, daily
amount, retry position, self-check labels, or any guaranteed retention, exam,
writing, reading, or speaking outcome.
